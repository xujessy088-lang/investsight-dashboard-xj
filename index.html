    03 / Household associations

    Which characteristics are associated with holding?

    Adjusted odds ratios from an explanatory analysis of training households.

    Overall training sample · fixed view

    A broader household profile

    Points: adjusted odds ratios · lines: 95% confidence intervals

    Logarithmic axis. OR = 1 means no adjusted association. Units and reference groups differ; this is not a feature-importance ranking. Twenty substantive terms with nominal p < .05 are displayed; the significant credit-card missingness indicator is omitted.

    How to read it

    1.59× adjusted odds

    A one-level increase in financial attention is associated with 1.59 times the odds of current ownership, holding other fitted terms fixed.

    95% CI: 1.49–1.70

    This does not mean a 59% increase in probability.

    Conversation design

    Check familiarity and preferences.

    Use attention, product awareness and stated risk preference as questions to ask. Test simpler explanations and assisted review where useful.

    A group profile does not establish any individual household's needs.

    Scope of the evidence

    Association, not a promised outcome.

    The forest plot stays the same when the group or capacity changes. It uses a separate unpenalised Logit on training data, not the deployed L1 coefficients.

    SD = standard deviation. Binary numeric inputs use per-SD units, rather than yes vs no. Age terms must be read jointly. Debt uses the asinh transformation. Intervals are nominal, without multiplicity correction.

    04 / From insight to a pilot

    Test the workflow before claiming business value.

    Use these observations to design a pilot; measure what happens after contact.

    01

    Set a workable capacity.

    Use staff time and review effort to choose list size. The 10% example is a scenario, not a proven optimum.

    02

    Tailor the conversation.

    Confirm needs and understanding. Offer assisted channels alongside digital ones; do not exclude households solely by group or score.

    03

    Compare real outcomes.

    Compare model-ranked and equal-size random review groups under the same process. Track response, completed reviews and effort; assess conversion separately if that becomes the pilot objective.
