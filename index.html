01 / Household characteristics

Household Profile

Which characteristics are associated with current investment ownership?

Training-set explanatory analysis
Read the finding

Financial engagement is an informative signal.

Each additional level of financial attention is associated with 1.59× adjusted odds of current ownership (95% CI 1.49–1.70). Product awareness has an OR of 1.43 per standard deviation.

Pilot action

Ask about product familiarity; tailor the explanation and check understanding.

Read the finding

Preferences and location give context.

Risk preference and urban residence are associated with ownership after adjustment for the other fitted terms. These are group-level patterns to discuss.

Pilot action

Confirm each household's needs and stated risk preference. Keep assisted review available across locations.

Education & communication

Use accessible explanations.

Each additional education level is associated with 1.30× adjusted odds (95% CI 1.23–1.39). This does not establish an effect of education programmes.

Pilot action

Use plain language and check comprehension across education levels. Test whether tailored communication improves understanding.

The forest plot uses a separate unpenalised Logit fitted on training data, rather than the deployed L1 coefficients. It stays fixed when the household group or review capacity changes. OR is an odds multiplier, not a probability multiplier. Binary numeric terms use per-SD units; age terms are read jointly; debt uses the asinh transformation. Confidence intervals are nominal, without multiplicity correction.

02 / Income and ownership

Income contributes a non-linear signal.

Income effect reproduced from the saved final LR model, with other inputs held fixed.

Read the finding

Use income alongside household needs.

The fitted relationship changes across the income range. Income is one input in a broader household profile.

Pilot action

Combine income context with financial engagement and preferences. Confirm individual circumstances before planning a review.

The curve does not establish a service cutoff or show that increasing income would cause ownership.

    04 / From insight to action

    Test the workflow before claiming business value.

    Use the findings to design a pilot and measure what happens after review.

    01

    Set a workable capacity.

    Use staff time and review effort to choose list size. The 10% example is a scenario, not a proven optimum.

    02

    Tailor the conversation.

    Confirm needs, product familiarity and understanding. Keep assisted channels available across household groups.

    03

    Measure real outcomes.

    Compare model-ranked and equal-size random review groups under the same process. Track response, completed reviews and effort; evaluate conversion separately if it becomes the pilot objective.
