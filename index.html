Why retain the LR baseline?

Ensemble models did not clear the project's prespecified combined criteria for stable CV and material validation improvement.

LR provides additive explanations, a small saved model (about 11 KB) and fewer dependencies. CatBoost remains the boosting challenger.

Capacity and threshold answer different questions.

Top-N: review a fixed number of the highest-ranked households. Threshold: flag every household with probability at or above a cutoff.

Probability cutoffHouseholds flaggedHolders foundPrecisionRecall

These operating points were chosen on training OOF predictions and evaluated on validation data. At 0.323, 228 households are flagged; the top-10% capacity list has exactly 215 households. The lower cutoff targeted 80% recall on training OOF predictions and achieved 75.1% on validation.

Data sources, definitions and reproducibility

Target: current ownership of risky financial investment. A household is positive when it has positive market value in stocks, bonds, funds, derivatives or financial wealth-management products. Account opening and uninvested cash alone do not define a positive label.

Metrics: precision = holders found / households reviewed; recall = holders found / all actual holders. PR-AUC here is average precision (AP), a whole-model ranking metric, not a household score.

Planner: final saved LR probabilities on the validation split, sorted in descending order with stable tie ordering. For each percentage, the number reviewed is 2,151 × percentage rounded to the nearest whole household. Random expectations are averages, not fractional observed households. Curves connect observed integer-percentage capacities.
